      League Keeper Rules

      Number of keepers

      Each team may keep up to 2 players from their previous season's roster.

      Three-season term limit

      A player can be on a roster for a maximum of 3 total seasons, counting the season they were acquired (draft, trade, or free agency). After the third season the player returns to the general draft pool.

      Acquired for 2025 → eligible 2025, 2026, 2027 → released for 2028.

      Keeper cost

      Players drafted in the 1st or 2nd round always cost a 1st-round pick, every season.

      Everyone else — 1st keeper season costs round − 1. 2nd keeper season costs previous cost − 2.

      Drafted R5 → kept for a 4th → kept for a 2nd → back to the pool.

      Waiver wire — the "OBJ Rule"

      A player picked up off waivers costs one round higher than their ADP in the upcoming draft. Per the rulebook's example, a 2nd-round ADP costs a 3rd-round pick.

      An ADP of Round 10 or later costs only a final-round pick.

      Waiver eligibility

      The player must have been on your roster for at least 3 consecutive active play weeks. They do not need to have started.

      Injury exception

      If a player was rostered the entire season but accrued points in 3 games or fewer due to injury, that season does not count toward the three-season limit. Keep in the IR slot.
