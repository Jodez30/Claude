League keeper rules
  • 2 keepers per team, max, from last season's roster.
  • 3 seasons on a roster, max — the year they were acquired counts as season 1. That means a player can be kept twice, then returns to the pool.
  • Rounds 1–2 originals always cost a 1st to keep, every season, no exceptions.
  • Everyone else: first keeper season costs the original round − 1; second costs that − 2. Drafted in the 5th → 4th → 2nd → pool.
  • Waiver pickups (OBJ Rule): cost is one round later than the player's ADP round. ADP of the 10th or later is capped at the final round. Must have been rostered 3+ consecutive active weeks.
  • Injury exception: a season where a rostered player scored in 3 or fewer games due to injury doesn't count toward the 3-season cap. Must be kept in the IR slot, and must be confirmed manually — the app only flags candidates.
